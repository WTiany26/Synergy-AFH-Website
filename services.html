What We Offer

Pillars of Excellent Care

Get Started Today
01
Transitional Placement

We thoughtfully match your loved one to the right Synergy home based on clinical needs, personality, and preferences. No guesswork. No middlemen. Direct provider connections only — always.

02
Medical Oversight

Provider-led monitoring including nurse delegation, pharmacy coordination, in-home physician visits, and chronic disease management — every single day, without exception.

03
Hospice Care Support

Compassionate end-of-life care in a home environment — comfort, dignity, and peace for residents and their families during the most tender and important times.

04
Medication Administration

Accurate, professional medication management by trained caregivers under nurse delegation. Your loved one's prescriptions are tracked, administered, and documented — never missed.

05
Respite Care

Short-term, high-quality care relief for family caregivers. We step in so you can rest, travel, or attend to life — with complete peace of mind that your loved one is in excellent hands.

06
Social Engagement

Structured daily activities, outings, appointment accompaniment, and meaningful social connection — because thriving means far more than simply surviving day to day.

How It Works

Your Journey with Synergy

From first call to first day — we walk with you every step of the way.

I
Initial Consultation

We listen to understand your loved one's full picture — medical needs, personality, preferences, and timeline.

II
Clinical Matching

Our team identifies the ideal Synergy home and caregiver based on thorough clinical and personal compatibility assessment.

III
Home Visit & Meeting

Tour the home, meet caregivers in person, and experience the environment. No pressure — your confidence comes first.

IV
Seamless Transition

We coordinate every detail of the move-in, with dedicated family support before, during, and after.
