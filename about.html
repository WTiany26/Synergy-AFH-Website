Our Story

The Vision Behind Synergy

Synergy AFH Providers was founded by twelve dedicated adult family home providers in Washington State — each bringing decades of hands-on nursing experience and a firsthand understanding of the gaps in senior care. United by a commitment to dignity, equity, and personalized attention, we came together with one purpose: to redefine what exceptional senior care looks like for every family.

Our approach goes far beyond placement. We create, develop, and implement care services tailored to each individual resident — connecting families directly with experienced providers who understand their loved one's unique needs.

  • Whole-person approach — physical, emotional and social wellbeing
  • Direct connection with providers, eliminating placement middlemen
  • Partnered with social workers, case managers and hospital teams
  • Every care plan individually tailored, never templated
  • Licensed, insured and continuously audited for quality

The Synergy Difference

What Makes Us Different

We go far beyond finding placement. Synergy means real, hands-on, personalized care — no middlemen, no guesswork.

01
Direct Provider Connection

You speak directly with the providers who will care for your loved one — not a call center, not a referral agent. Complete transparency from day one, every day.

02
Clinically-Led Matching

Our placement process is driven by clinical compatibility — medical needs, dietary requirements, personality, and daily routines — ensuring the right fit, every time.

03
Zero Placement Fees

Because we are the providers, families pay no agency or middleman fees. Every dollar goes directly into the quality of care your loved one receives.

04
Rigorously Vetted Caregivers

Every caregiver undergoes comprehensive background checks, professional training, and ongoing supervision. Quality is never negotiable at Synergy.
